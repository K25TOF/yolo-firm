- Hypothesis (specific and testable)

- Dependencies (what must be true first)
- Estimated complexity: Low / Medium / High
Order by highest expected EV toward finding a reliably profitable strategy first.

Guiding principle: targeted strategies with real edge on specific trade types. A strategy that works selectively on 20 trades/month at 70% WR is more valuable than 200 trades at 52%.
