- **HHI calculation:** Top-20 tickers = 10.1% of 782 = ~79 runner-days. If distributed evenly across 20 tickers, each contributes ~4 runner-days (0.5%). HHI_top20 ≈ 20 × (0.005)² = 0.0005.
