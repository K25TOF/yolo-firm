This is the most important question given the core finding from LC-2025-005 (vol_filter loses broadly, wins only on outlier runners).

### RVOL Threshold Evidence

| Source | Finding | Confidence |
|---|---|---|

| EXP-019 | RVOL <5.0x would add moderate performers but also noise | Medium |
| EXP-020 | ~27 scanner-only tickers (RVOL ≥5x) produced near-zero PnL most days | Medium |

**Observation:** RVOL threshold creates a paradox. Higher threshold (5x+) filters out some profitable tickers (NPT at 3.1x RTH RVOL). **RVOL is necessary but not sufficient.** It identifies stocks with unusual activity but cannot distinguish sustained momentum from spike-and-fade.
