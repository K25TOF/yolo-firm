## PO HYPOTHESIS (share verbatim)

Runners on high-gap catalyst days follow recognisable patterns in the first 15-30 minutes of RTH. Four scenarios:

**Scenario 1 — Straight runner (rare):** Stock gaps up and keeps running after open with no meaningful pullback. Requires exceptionally strong catalyst.

**Scenario 2 — Gap, spike, pullback, ORB breakout:** Stock shoots up briefly after open, pulls back below opening price, finds support (e.g. pre-market low), reverses, tests previous high, then breaks out and runs.

**Scenario 3 — Gap, immediate tank, reversal, ORB breakout:** Stock tanks immediately from open as holders take profit. Finds a floor, reverses, tests open price level, breaks through and runs.

**Scenario 4 — Failed runner:** Starts like Scenario 2 or 3 but never recovers.

**Why current entry fails:** Static ORB windows don't recognise which scenario is playing out. They enter at fixed time regardless of pattern — sometimes catching the spike just before pullback, sometimes missing the move entirely.
