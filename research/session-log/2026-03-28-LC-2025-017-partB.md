### On Challenge 2 (Bad Label — Lookahead Bias Risk)

Challenger is right that MAE-based Bad labels carry lookahead risk *if used as entry filters*. But they are not being proposed as entry filters — they're being proposed as **agreement-rate proxies** against existing PO ratings. The question is: "does this mechanical definition reproduce what PO called Bad?"

The lookahead concern is real but limited in this context because:
1. We're not training a forward model on them — we're measuring label concordance
2. PO's visual rating *also* uses forward information (the PO sees the full trade chart)
3. The goal is to find a definition that a future strategy can apply at close of trade (not at entry time)

However, Challenger's point about threshold arbitrariness is correct. **The right approach is cross-validation against PO ratings, not pre-specifying a threshold.**

**Revised Direction 2 proposal:**
- Test 3–5 MAE% thresholds (3%, 5%, 7%, 10%) and 3 MFE% thresholds (2%, 3%, 5%) against PO's 298 labels
- Report the concordance curve, not just a single threshold
- Accept the threshold with highest concordance AND ≥80% precision on "Bad" (we care more about not mislabelling Good as Bad than the reverse)
- **Critical constraint:** Any mechanical Bad label must be flagged as a *post-hoc outcome proxy*, not an entry-time filter. Entry-time filters require a separate derivation

The six directions are asking fundamentally different questions about different constructs (entry quality, label definition, OOS validity, exit optimisation, stop sizing, price pattern). They are not independent tests of the *same hypothesis* — they are a research programme testing different hypotheses. Family-wise error rate (FWER) correction (Bonferroni) is designed for simultaneous tests of the same null hypothesis. Applying it across six distinct research questions would be overly conservative and methodologically unjustified.

**However, within-direction corrections are required where multiple variants are tested against the same outcome:**
- D1A: No correction needed (quintile stratification is descriptive, not hypothesis-testing per quintile)
- D2: BH correction across 15 MAE/MFE combinations ✅
- D4: Bonferroni across 3 pairwise EMA comparisons ✅
- D5: Bonferroni across 3 stop thresholds (moot — underpowered regardless)
