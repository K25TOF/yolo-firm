## Known Sources

### Academic / Practitioner (higher quality)

- **Hill, Arthur (SSRN:3412429, Feb 2019)** — "Finding Consistent Trends with Strong Momentum: RSI for Trend-Following and Momentum Strategies." Tests RSI range signals on S&P 500 stocks. Key finding: RSI bull range (RSI between 40–100) captures trend consistency; a break below 40 signals exit from bull range. Describes 40 as the lower bound of a "bull range" — directly relevant as Guard C uses RSI<40 as its exit trigger. *Practitioner-academic hybrid; SSRN, not peer-reviewed journal.*

- **Panigrahi, Ashok (SSRN:3986000, Feb 2022)** — RSI 60/40 thresholds on NIFTY 50. Finds 60-40 strategy outperforms 50-50 for short-term returns. RSI<40 as exit threshold has documented practitioner-academic support. *SSRN working paper.*

- **Tyagi, Vipul (SSRN:5386654, 2025)** — RSI 60/40 integrated with Dow Theory trend confirmation. Relevant to "RSI below 40 = exit" logic combined with trend indicator (conceptually similar to Guard C's EMA5 component). *SSRN working paper.*

- **irjmets.com study (June 2024)** — RSI 40/60 thresholds tested on Indian equities. Selling when RSI falls below 40 described as a mechanism to "exit as a stock is falling and prevents getting caught in prolonged downtrends." Supports Guard C's RSI<40 exit logic. *Low-tier journal; treat as practitioner-level only.*

- **Newfound Research / Think New Found (2018)** — "Protect & Participate: Managing Drawdowns with Trend Following." Defines "crisis" as >25% drawdown. Trend following (moving average based) reduces drawdown in crisis periods. Conceptually supports a price-vs-MA guard. *Practitioner/blog; not peer-reviewed.*

- **Alpha Architect (2021)** — "Avoiding the Big Drawdown with Trend-Following." Rule: if weak absolute/trending performance, go to cash. Conceptual support for drawdown guards. *Practitioner paper.*

- **CFA Institute Digest (2013)** — Summary of Bajgrowicz & Scaillet: technical trading rules have no economic value after transaction costs since 1962 (even at zero cost). IMPORTANT CONFLICT with any claim that RSI/EMA rules provide durable alpha. *High quality; peer-reviewed digest.*

- **TradingView/EdgeTools study (undated, cited as 15M tests)** — Comprehensive test of RSI overbought/oversold signals: "RSI extreme readings provide no statistically significant predictive power after accounting for multiple testing." Notes institutional use is as momentum filter (RSI>50 = stay long, RSI<50 = caution) not mean-reversion signal. *Practitioner/informal; not peer-reviewed but large-scale.*

- **QuantifiedStrategies.com / Hill-inspired RSI range-momentum** — RSI ranges 40–80 during uptrends; "during a normal pullback, RSI finds support and reverses in the 40–50 zone. A move to 30 suggests something more than just a pullback." Supports 40 as a meaningful break threshold. *Practitioner backtest blog.*

- **Maróy, Ákos (SSRN:5095349, Jan 2025)** — Intraday momentum exit strategies: VWAP and ladder-based exits outperform simple indicator exits. Not directly about RSI/EMA guards but shows exit strategy choice matters significantly. *SSRN.*
