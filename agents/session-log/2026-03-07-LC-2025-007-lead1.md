## Mandatory Audit Gates

### Gate (a) — Hold Duration Distribution

**Observation:** Avg hold 4.9 bars reported. Expected grinder profile: 15–30 bars.

I cannot compute the full hold distribution without the CSV. However:
- 4.9 bars avg is inconsistent with a grinder profile by construction. Even if a meaningful tail of trades held 15+ bars, they would need to be very numerous to pull the average to 4.9 from a base of 15–30. The opposite is true — short-hold trades are dominating.
- For context: vol_filter baseline avg hold is 7.5 bars (EXP-022). Grinder at 4.9 bars is *shorter* than vol_filter. This is the opposite of the expected differentiation.
- EXP-021 showed trades holding 8+ bars won 83% of the time; trades exiting in 1–3 bars lost 88%. At 4.9 bars average, a large fraction of these 12,024 trades are in the loser-dominated <5 bar bucket.

**Gate (a) verdict: FAIL.** Grinder leg profile is not confirmed. The exit at `crosses_below 0.5%` is almost certainly firing within 1–5 bars of entry for the majority of trades, meaning the entry at 1.0% creates a position that decays to 0.5% quickly — not a sustained grinder leg. This is a structural mismatch between exit threshold and expected hold duration.

---

### Gate (b) — PnL Concentration (Principle #6)

**Observation:** +1,889.2% total PnL, 32.5% WR, 12,024 trades.

Arithmetic check:
- 32.5% of 12,024 = ~3,908 winners, ~8,116 losers
- If losers average ~-2% each (consistent with 4.9 bar hold profile): 8,116 × -2% = -162.3% aggregate loss contribution
- For total to be +1,889.2%, winners must average approximately: (1,889.2 + 162.3) / 3,908 ≈ **+52.7% per winning trade**

That is an extraordinary average winner. For comparison, vol_filter's avg winner across EXP-023 was in the low single digits. A +52.7% average winner on 3,908 trades implies either:
1. A handful of extreme outlier trades (hundreds of percent) are carrying the aggregate, or
2. The PnL calculation has a mechanical issue (e.g., leveraged or compounded PnL)

I cannot determine which without the CSV, but the arithmetic strongly suggests outlier dependency. The mean winner magnitude required to produce +1,889% total at 32.5% WR is not consistent with a disciplined grinder strategy.

**Gate (b) verdict: STRONG SUSPICION of Principle #6 violation.** Cannot confirm without CSV distribution, but the arithmetic demands either extreme outliers or a calculation artifact. Flag to Manager for CSV review before any verdict.

---

### Gate (c) — Skip-First at Scale

**Observation:** 12,024 trades across 5,709 pairs, 183 dates. That is 65.7 trades/day, ~2.1 trades per pair.

Skip-first logic: the *first* EMA gap cross on a ticker-date pair is skipped; only the second+ fires. If skip-first is functioning:
- 25,680 no-VWAP trades across 5,709 pairs = ~4.5 signals per pair
- After skip-first + VWAP filter: 12,024 trades — plausible, roughly 2.1 trades per pair
- The ratio (12,024 / 25,680 = 46.8% of no-VWAP trades) is broadly consistent with skip-first removing first signals plus VWAP filtering additional entries

However, 65.7 trades/day is markedly higher than EXP-023's avg of 5.5 trades/day. The difference is universe scale (5,709 pairs vs ~40 pairs in EXP-023). At this scale, skip-first is operating per ticker-date pair, which is correct by design.

**Gate (c) verdict: CONDITIONALLY PASS.** Skip-first appears mechanically functional (trade count ratio is consistent). However, I flag a conceptual concern: at 183-date scale with momentum universe filtering, "skip-first" across thousands of tickers per day is not the same trading regime as EXP-022's selective 6-day skip-first on 28 trades. The skip-first insight was validated on ~40 tickers; here it operates across hundreds. Whether the signal quality holds at this breadth requires CSV-level ticker concentration analysis.

### Flag 2 — Avg Hold 4.9 Bars

**I observe:** Exit at `crosses_below 0.5%` fires quickly after entry at 1.0%.

Root cause reasoning: the EMA gap (3,9) is a fast-decaying indicator. A gap that crosses 1.0% upward can return to 0.5% in 3–6 bars as price consolidates. This is a design problem, not a tuning problem. The 0.5% exit threshold was chosen by analogy to vol_filter's ratio (entry 3.0%, exit 1.5% — ratio 0.5). Applied to grinder (entry 1.0%, exit 0.5% — ratio 0.5), the absolute gap of 0.5% is too narrow for the EMA(3,9) to hold during normal consolidation.

**This suggests the exit threshold is wrong for the grinder entry level.** A grinder entry at 1.0% with exit at 0.5% leaves only 0.5pp of gap decay before exit fires. Vol_filter's entry at 3.0% with exit at 1.5% leaves 1.5pp of decay room. The grinder needs more exit headroom relative to its entry level, not the same proportional ratio.

**Conclusion on Flag 2:** Exit at 0.5% is architecturally too tight for a 1.0% entry in the EMA(3,9) space. This is a material config concern that should be documented regardless of verdict.

### Flag 3 — PnL Concentration

**Addressed in Gate (b) above.** +1,889.2% at 32.5% WR on 12,024 trades requires avg winning trade of ~+52.7%. This is arithmetic evidence of extreme outlier dependency. **Cannot confirm without CSV distribution, but the numbers demand it.**
