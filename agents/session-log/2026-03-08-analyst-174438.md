## Verdict

**The time-decay thesis does not hold under scrutiny.**

Three disconfirming findings:

1. **2h-news trades underperform control (no-news).** -0.885% vs -0.691% avg loss. News within 2 hours of entry is a negative signal, not positive. This is the opposite of the intended filter.

2. **Stale news (2–24h) is worse than fresh news (0–2h).** The divergent set (-0.878%) is worse than the 24h baseline (-0.565%), confirming time-decay exists. But this supports *including* stale news, not excluding it — the best trades come from the mixed pool, not the 2h subset alone.

3. **Total PnL is misleading due to trade reduction.** Going from 6,565 trades at -0.691%/trade to 1,699 trades at -0.133%/trade looks like improvement because total loss shrinks (-225.8% vs -4,531.0%). But the 1,699 remaining trades are still net-negative. The apparent improvement is just loss-reduction through filtering, not a true edge. The filtered-out trades (-2,064.6%) contain most of the original loss, which masks the fact that the remaining pool is *still* underwater.

## Why the Signal Reversed

**Hypothesis (failing):** News recency improves entry quality → tighter 2h window should outperform 24h window.

**Actual finding:** News within 2h of entry correlates with *worse* trades. Possible explanations:

1. **Timing lag in news delivery.** Benzinga news timestamp may represent publication time (2–6h after event), not event occurrence time. An entry 2h after publication could be 4–8h after the event, by which time the initial spike has already reversed.

2. **News-driven reversals.** Stocks that trend on news momentum often reverse sharply within the first 2–4 hours (spike-and-fade). Entries within 2h of news publication catch the *reversal phase*, not the momentum phase. Entries 4–24h later catch either the secondary leg or the post-reversal stabilization (which may hold better).

3. **False signal from single indicator.** News presence is not a directional signal by itself. It marks *events*, not *direction*. A news trigger on bad earnings is the same signal as on good earnings from a pure pattern-matching perspective. Without valence (positive/negative news), the filter is adding noise.

4. **EXP-024 revisited.** Earlier research showed news presence improved WR by only +1.3pp. The current finding (news actually *worsens* results) may indicate that in the broader universe, news-correlated entries are predominantly negative-valence events (downgrades, offerings, legal issues), not positive catalysts.
