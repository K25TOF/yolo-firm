## Observation vs Conclusion

**Observation:** News-presence filter removes 38.3% of trades (2,515 of 6,565), eliminating a slightly lower-quality subset (28.77% WR, -0.891% avg PnL). Aggregate WR and PnL both improve modestly.

**Conclusion:** The filter is **directionally valid** — it identifies trades with marginally lower quality. However, the improvement is **not material enough to recommend adoption** as a hard entry gate.

- **Why?** The strategy is net-negative across all 192 dates on the momentum universe. A +0.50pp WR improvement and +0.126pp avg PnL improvement are helpful but do not address the core problem: the strategy loses money on most tickers/days.
- **Trade-off cost:** 2,515 removed trades represent potential upside in outlier scenarios (if the strategy were profitable overall). On a strategy that's losing, the cost is small, but the benefit is equally small.

---

## Actionable Assessment

**For scanner/entry pipeline enrichment:** News presence is a **valid soft filter** — use it to rank entries or as a secondary tiebreaker (prefer news-backed entries when multiple signals fire). Do not use as a hard rejection gate; cost is low, benefit is marginal.

**Example implementation:** In live trading, if two tickers fire entry signals simultaneously and position limits allow only one trade, prefer the ticker with `has_news_24h > 0`.

**Not recommended as:** Standalone filter to block low-news trades. The 38.3% trade reduction does not improve profitability enough to justify loss of potential outlier wins.
